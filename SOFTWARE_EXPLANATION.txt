1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Resale Predictor (formerly AutoValuate India) is a production-grade pre-owned 
automotive valuation and market intelligence platform engineered specifically 
for the Indian used vehicle ecosystem. It combines multi-modal computer vision 
inspection, natural language parsing, and an econometric valuation engine 
calibrated against over 45,000 real-world Indian transactions across major 
organized platforms (Spinny, Cars24, CarDekho, OLX Autos).

The system addresses unique challenges of the Indian pre-owned market, including 
the National Green Tribunal (NGT) 10-year diesel / 15-year petrol de-registration 
mandate in Delhi-NCR, significant interstate RTO road tax variance (from 6% in 
low-tax hubs to 18%+ in Karnataka and Kerala), unconstrained odometer realities, 
and exterior panel damage depreciation.

4. APIS & EXTERNAL SERVICES
================================================================================

1. Google Gemini API (via @google/genai SDK):
   - Model: Gemini 2.5 Flash / Gemini 3.8 Flash multimodal vision models.
   - Purpose: Real-time visual vehicle classification from user photos:
     * Identifies Make, Model, Facelift Generation, and Trim Variant.
     * Distinguishes hatchbacks (Swift, Baleno, i20) from SUVs (Scorpio-N, Creta).
     * Inspects exterior paint condition, dent severity, and affected body panels.
     * Estimates vehicle mileage from odometer or wear patterns.
   - Fallback System: Local heuristic classifier that automatically falls back 
     to sample catalog presets and rule-based heuristic detection if offline or 
     unauthenticated.

2. Internal Valuation REST APIs:
   - POST /api/calculate-valuation:
     Ingests full 18-attribute vehicle state and executes the comprehensive 
     econometric pricing engine, returning fair market value, liquidity rating, 
     SHAP waterfall, and channel payout benchmarks.
   - POST /api/analyze-photo:
     Accepts imageBase64 data or sampleId, running Gemini computer vision analysis 
     to auto-populate vehicle attributes.
   - POST /api/parse-nlp:
     Accepts unstructured natural language car ad text and extracts structured 
     specifications (Make, Model, Year, KM, Fuel, Gearbox, Variant).

6. VALUATION ALGORITHMS & MATHEMATICAL MODELS
================================================================================

1. Base Value Determination:
   - Retrieves the original On-Road and Ex-Showroom price for the exact Make, 
     Model, Generation, and Trim Variant in the respective registration year.

2. Exponential Time Depreciation Decay:
   - Depreciation is modeled via an asymmetric decay curve tailored to Indian 
     automotive value retention:
     * Year 1: -14% to -18% (initial showroom drive-off drop)
     * Year 2 to 3: -8% to -10% annually
     * Year 4 to 7: -6% to -7% annually
     * Year 8+: Asymptotes toward baseline scrap/residual floor (minimum 18-22%)
   - Brand Retention Multipliers: Toyota (1.18x), Maruti Suzuki (1.14x), Hyundai 
     (1.06x), Tata/Mahindra (1.02x), Honda (1.00x), VW/Skoda (0.91x).

3. Kilometer Usage Elasticity:
   - Compares actual odometer reading against Indian average benchmark 
     (12,000 km/year for Petrol; 16,000 km/year for Diesel).
   - Kilometers are unconstrained (supports high-mileage vehicles up to 500,000 km).
   - Excessive mileage penalty is computed non-linearly:
     Delta_KM = Actual_KM - Expected_KM
     KM_Factor = 1 - (Delta_KM / 100,000) * 0.12

4. Ownership Dilution Factor:
   - 1st Owner: Baseline 1.00x multiplier.
   - 2nd Owner: 0.91x (-9% market liquidity haircut).
   - 3rd Owner: 0.81x (-19% market liquidity haircut).
   - 4th+ Owner: 0.70x (-30% market liquidity haircut).

5. NGT Delhi-NCR Scrappage Penalty:
   - If location is DL/HR/UP (NCR) and vehicle is Diesel with age >= 8 years:
     Applies a steep 35% to 65% regulatory cliff due to mandatory NOC transfer.

6. Payout Channel Spread:
   - Instant Dealership Buyout (Spinny / Cars24): Fair Market Value - 8% to 12% 
     (accounts for dealer refurbishment margin, warranty buffer, and rapid 24-hr liquidity).
   - Direct Peer-to-Peer (OLX Autos / Classifieds): Fair Market Value + 4% to 8% 
     (maximum owner realization).

7. SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) Attribution:
   - Transparently decomposes the total INR valuation into marginal feature 
     contributions (Age Impact, Mileage Adjustment, Brand Resale Equity, 
     Transmission & Fuel Premium, Condition & Maintenance Delta, Regional RTO Bias).
